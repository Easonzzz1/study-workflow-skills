# Synthetic source: ratios and percentages

This material was written for this repository. All quantities are fictional. It is not a course handout, an exam paper, or a record about real people.

## Ratios

A ratio compares quantities. If a fictional box contains 3 blue counters and 2 white counters, the ratio blue:white is 3:2. There are 5 counters in total, so the blue fraction of all counters is 3/5. The ratio of blue to white, 3/2, uses a different denominator from the fraction of all counters.

## Percentages

A percentage is a fraction expressed per hundred: percentage = part / whole × 100%. In the fictional box, blue counters make up (3/5) × 100% = 60% of all counters. White counters make up 40%.

## Change

If a fictional quantity rises from 20 to 25, its absolute change is 5 and its relative increase is (25 − 20)/20 × 100% = 25%. The initial value is the denominator. A change from 40% to 50% is 10 percentage points, but a relative increase of 25%.
